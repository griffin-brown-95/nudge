Just pick one.

Quick choices. No right answers.

Every pair you saw was styled at random: the color, how filled-in each button was, its size, which one came first, whether it had a label, and sometimes which one was pre-selected.

We're measuring which of those nudge people's picks. Results will be shared once there's enough data.

Picks are logged anonymously for a fun experiment. Your browser keeps a random ID so you can pick up where you left off. No names, no IP addresses, no cookies.
