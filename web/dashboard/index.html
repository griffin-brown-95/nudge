How people decide

Randomization health

Each factor should be assigned in equal shares. A lopsided split (a "sample ratio mismatch") would mean a bug in assignment or logging.

Method

  • Ranges are 95% Wilson score intervals. A result appears once it has at least 30 trials; until then only its count is shown.
  • Each visitor's first view of each trial counts. Trials played after reading the "what was going on" explanation are excluded.
  • Levels are randomized independently per option, per trial and per visitor with salted hashes, so every pick informs every factor.
